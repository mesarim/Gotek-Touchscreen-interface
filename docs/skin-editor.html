Skin Editor

Your own look for the GTi screen, in one .gts file

A skin is a 256 × 192 picture (the atlas: keys, cards and the selected row), a few colours and, if you like, a background photo. Paint the atlas in any program, drop it in here and watch the real screens redraw with it. Nothing is uploaded: the page works on your computer only. Skins work on the JC3248 screen (the 3.5 inch GTi); the slots are explained in the skin guide.

Live preview

The preview is drawn with the same 9-slice rules, slot positions and layout as the firmware. Sample data: a FluxDrive with Xenon 2 in DF0 and DF1. Cover pictures, the reel, icons and the battery gauge are not drawn here.

    Put it on the screen

    This page cannot send the skin to your GTi: it is served over https and the GTi's own page is plain http, which a browser does not allow. So you do the last step yourself, in one of two ways.

    Over Wi-Fi: the GTi's own web page

    1. Download the .gts above.
    2. Open the GTi's page: http://gti-xxxx.local/panel (gti-xxxx is your GTi's own name on the network; use the address you already use for its web page).
    3. Go to Themes, open the Theme Editor and press Import skin (PNG or .gts). Pick your .gts. The panel checks it first and tells you in plain words if something is wrong.
    4. It shows up live. Activate it (Save & activate), or pick it on the screen: Settings → Screen → Theme.

    By SD card

    1. Download the .gts. Keep the file name upper case, A–Z, 0–9 and _ (the editor does that for you): MY_SKIN.gts.
    2. Copy it to /GTI/THEMES/ on the GTi's SD card (the screen creates the folder if it is missing).
    3. Put the card back. The skin is in the list at Settings → Screen → Theme. To start with it, put THEME=MY_SKIN in CONFIG.TXT.

    Firmware A600-skin1-JC3248 or later; the calm screens shown here need A600-calm1 or later. A skin cannot hurt: a slot that is empty is drawn the classic way and a file that does not fit is refused. Colour themes and skins share one list of 8 custom entries. More in the skin guide.
