GTi touchscreen: proposed layout (480 x 320, actual size)

One font, three sizes (16 / 24 / 32 px), every touch area at least 48 px (the drawing may be smaller, the touch area not), an 8 px grid. Always on screen: at the top where you are (List / Reel / Settings), at the bottom the drives. Browsing and choosing are split: the list only browses, the program screen has the actions. Eject only happens in Drive info, never by accident.

Other setups: the drive strip and the load key follow what is there

Most GTi screens sit directly in a Gotek (screen only) or send to one dongle: one drive, no DF0/DF1. The bottom strip and the load keys show "drives" (whatever can be reached): 1 drive = one wide card and one load key, 2 = two halves, 3 or more = a picker.
