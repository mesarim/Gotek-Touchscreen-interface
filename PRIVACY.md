# GTi — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 17 August 2026**

GTi ("the app") is published by OMEGAWARE (contact: mesarim@gmail.com). This
policy explains what information the app handles and what leaves your device.
The short version: **GTi collects no personal information, has no accounts, and
sends nothing about you to us — the only third party involved is Google's
advertising SDK, unless you remove ads.**

## What the app does with your files

GTi is a controller for Gotek/FlashFloppy-style floppy emulator hardware. You
grant the app access to a folder of disk-image files (ADF) on your device or
memory card. These files are read **locally** to build your library and are
transmitted **only** to your own emulator hardware ("dongle") over a direct
local WiFi connection that you configure. Your disk images, file names,
favourites, settings, and library data never leave your device except to reach
your own hardware, and are never sent to us or any server.

## Network access

The app uses your device's WiFi to connect to your own dongle hardware (by
default the dongle's own access point). Internet access is used solely for
serving advertisements (below). The app contains no analytics, tracking, or
telemetry of its own.

## Advertising (free version)

The free version shows a single small banner advertisement served by **Google
AdMob**. Like all mobile advertising SDKs, AdMob may collect device
identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP
address, and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. This collection is
performed by Google under Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

We have configured ads to a maximum content rating of PG and use only banner
format ads. We do not receive any of the data AdMob collects.

If you purchase the one-time "Remove ads" upgrade, the advertising SDK serves
no ads and the banner is removed.

## Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We
do not receive or store any payment details. The unlock state is stored
locally on your device.

## Data we store

All app data — your granted folder location, favourites, theme, intro
preference, dongle list, and remove-ads status — is stored locally on your
device and can be erased by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. We
operate no servers and hold no data about you.

## Children

GTi is a utility for retro-computing hardware and is not directed at children.
Ads are capped at a PG content rating.

## Your rights

Since we collect and hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to
provide, correct, or delete. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's
privacy policy and your device's ad-personalisation settings (you can reset or
delete your advertising ID in Android settings). EU/UK users: the legal basis
for ad serving is consent/legitimate interest as managed by Google; the app
works identically if ad personalisation is disabled at the device level.
